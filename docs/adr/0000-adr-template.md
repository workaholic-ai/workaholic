# ADR 0000: Concise Decision Title

- Status: Proposed
- Decision date: Record the date in ISO 8601 format.
- Deciders: Name the accountable people or roles.
- Supersedes: None
- Superseded by: None

## Context

Describe the forces, constraints, and problem that require a durable decision.
Include enough evidence for a reader who did not participate in the original
discussion. Link related scope, threat-model, and contract documents.

## Decision

State the decision in direct, testable terms. Define affected interfaces,
invariants, and ownership boundaries. Separate requirements from examples.

## Alternatives considered

Describe credible alternatives and why they were not selected. Include the
option of retaining the current design when relevant.

## Consequences

Document positive and negative consequences, operational constraints, follow-up
work, and conditions that would justify revisiting the decision.

## References

Link repository documents, issues, pull requests, or authoritative external
sources that materially support the decision.

---

Use exactly one status:

- `Proposed`: under review and not yet binding.
- `Accepted`: approved and binding.
- `Superseded`: replaced by another ADR; set `Superseded by`.
- `Rejected`: considered and explicitly not adopted.

An accepted ADR is immutable historical context. Change a decision by adding a
new ADR and marking the old record `Superseded`; do not rewrite the old decision
to make history appear consistent.
