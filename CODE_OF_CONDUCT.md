# Workaholic AI Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Workaholic AI contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
safe, constructive, and accessible. We welcome people with different
backgrounds, identities, abilities, experience levels, and viewpoints.

Participation in this project is a privilege conditioned on professional,
respectful behavior.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking the person presenting them;
- give specific, actionable feedback and receive it in good faith;
- acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and learn from their impact;
- respect privacy, personal boundaries, and requests to stop an interaction;
- keep project spaces focused on the work and the health of the community; and
- alert maintainers when they observe unsafe or unacceptable conduct.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or unwanted attention;
- discriminatory, degrading, or sexualized language or imagery;
- trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- pressuring anyone to reveal credentials, private communications, or personal
  information; and
- retaliation against a person who raises a concern or assists an
  investigation.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository activity, issue and pull-request
conversations, project communications, and events. It also applies when someone
is representing Workaholic AI through an official account, address, or role.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to
[pg@ithesion.com](mailto:pg@ithesion.com) with the subject `Workaholic AI
conduct report`. Do not open a public issue when doing so could expose a
reporter, affected person, or private information.

Include only the context needed to understand the incident: where it occurred,
when it occurred, the behavior involved, and any relevant public links. Do not
send credentials, private keys, production data, or unrelated personal
information. The maintainer will limit disclosure to people needed to review
and resolve the report.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate private process in the
[security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review reports as promptly and impartially as practical,
ask for clarification when needed, and consider context, severity, recurrence,
and impact. Possible responses include:

1. a private correction and request to change behavior;
2. a formal warning with conditions on further participation;
3. a temporary restriction from project interactions; or
4. permanent removal from project spaces.

Immediate restrictions may be applied when necessary to protect participants
or preserve evidence. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports are themselves a
violation. Good-faith reports that cannot be substantiated are not.

Enforcement decisions and sensitive report details are kept private to the
extent practical. Public moderation notices may identify the rule and action
taken without exposing reporters or unnecessary personal information.
